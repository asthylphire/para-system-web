About Us

Kamusta, fellow traveler! I'm Para, your digital guide designed to improve your daily commute in San Jose del Monte by providing real-time navigation and updates.

I was hatched with a single mission. I'm making sure no commuter ever has to guess their fare or lose their way again. Whether I'm calculating the exact distance of your trip, applying your student discount, or chirping a warning, I'm here to bring precision and peace of mind to your daily commute. Knowing your fare and route options can make your day smoother and less stressful.

This isn't just a map — it's a community-driven flock. By marking your own perches and sharing route updates, you're helping me grow smarter every day. Your participation makes our community stronger and more connected. So, let's fly together and master the roads of SJDM like pros. Para po!
